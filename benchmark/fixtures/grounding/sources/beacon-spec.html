Beacon protocol specification

Beacon is the fictional discovery protocol of this fixture corpus. The text is Tangle-authored for the grounding benchmark and specifies no real wire.

A Beacon handshake proceeds in three stages: hello, verify, adopt. No stage may be skipped, and a peer that fails verify must restart from hello.

Every Beacon datagram carries a CRC32C checksum computed over both the header and the payload.

A connected Beacon peer emits a heartbeat every 20 seconds; three missed heartbeats retire the peer.

Beacon traffic travels over UDP on port 7432, one datagram per frame.
