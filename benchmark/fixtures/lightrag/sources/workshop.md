# Cedar Workshop records

Cedar Workshop teaches practical timber crafts.

Reed Mill prepares timber for restoration projects.

Slate Bridge connects the mill road to the town.

Opal Library lends books on design and construction.

Cedar Workshop obtains restoration timber from Reed Mill.

Reed Mill provides beams for the restoration of Slate Bridge.

Cedar Workshop repairs the wooden shelves of Opal Library.

CEDAR WORKSHOP is the uppercase register name of Cedar Workshop.

These records describe the fictional Windmere district. Entries are independent statements; a grant record never implies authority over an unrelated organization.
