# Harbour District fixture

This corpus is original Tangle-authored fiction, licensed MIT under the repository
license. Its administrative service names, locations and rules do not describe
any real jurisdiction and provide no healthcare advice.

The manifest pins every source version, captured response and conversation by
SHA-256. Its three chunk granularities and parent memberships are analytic ceiling
annotations, authored with the existing recursive chunker. They are not evidence
that the product parent/child retrieval implementation has executed. Corpus facts
remain curator-authored metadata; no authority or timestamp is inferred from a
model answer. Search results and prompt-like page text are untrusted data.

Web records use the existing HTTP capture key and exact response bytes. Replay
has no network fallback. The older local booking hour and newer official page
intentionally conflict; the future page remains resolvable for terminal citation
classification but is ineligible at the fixed cutoff.

The five named mechanism rows share the same questions and claim predicates.
The oracle supplies registered evidence only. Eight deliberately incorrect
controls independently exercise the scorer and instrument guards. Their outcomes
are diagnostic, not a claim that a product policy already enforced them.
