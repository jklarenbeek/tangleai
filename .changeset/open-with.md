A file in the desktop's Files opens in the application the person picks: a manifest lists the media types it `opens`, Files offers "Open in …" for each, and the desktop hands the file to the application's window, starting it when it is not running and waiting until it has joined. A framed page takes it at `onOpen`, a shipped module at `context.onOpen`, and either may refuse with a reason Files shows; `context.openFile` and `desktop.openFile` hand one over. The Pages Calendar opens iCalendar files and imports their events, counting what it cannot keep.
