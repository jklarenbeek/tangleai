MAS head activation is fenced by revision as well as version: `activateWorkflow` and `activateTemplate` take an optional `expectedRevision`, an applied activation and `getActiveWorkflow` return the head's `revision`, and a token read before the head moved away and back to the same version (A→B→A) is refused (`TMAS2001` at `/revision`). Without the revision the version alone decides, as before.
