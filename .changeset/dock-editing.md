The dock is the person's own document: in edit mode they move icons, drop one on another to make a folder, rename folders, take icons out, and hide and show them, by pointer, menu or keyboard, kept through the host's store (`createKernel({ dock })`). A manifest may list launch entries, each its own dock icon opening the module at one of its screens (`connection.screen`, `onScreen`), and name the folders its icons start in (`dock.folders`); an older desktop refuses a manifest with those members. The assistant reads the dock and suggests a new arrangement through the kernel's `tangle_os_dock` contract; the suggestion waits in the inbox, shown as its changes, until the person accepts it. Modules may no longer take the ids `shell`, `person` or `desktop`.
