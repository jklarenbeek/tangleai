The desktop's mail is the one inbox for what a person decides. An application declares the kinds of proposal it files (`proposals` in its manifest) and files them with `propose` (the same id replaces, `withdraw` takes one back); the mail shows them as text, with evidence linking only to http(s) pages, and the person accepts or rejects. The decision goes back to the application over its own control channel (`onDecision`), never as a contract operation, so neither the assistant nor another module can decide, whatever their grants. One decision runs at a time; an application that is not running or refuses (a moved revision) leaves the proposal pending with the reason. `kernel.propose`, `kernel.decide` and the `mail` events serve a host with its own inbox.
