Applications a person joins are installed with their consent. `kernel.join(manifest, { scopes, assistant, source })` installs one in the person's layer and grants exactly the scopes they allowed out of what its manifest asks for; what it provides, its header bar and its file location reach the assistant only when they said so, also on a desktop that grants the assistant everything. The joined applications, each with the origin, scopes and contracts the person was shown, are kept in the person's desktop state (`apps`), so they return on the next visit and on another device; `consentNeeded` says when to ask again (another origin, or a scope or contract they were not shown), `setConsent` changes it, and removing and undoing carry the consent along. `scopesOf`, `originOf` and `isLocalOrigin` give an install sheet what to show.
