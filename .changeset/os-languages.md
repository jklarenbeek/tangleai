The desktop's chrome speaks the eleven languages `@jarenjs/locales` ships besides English: `@tangleai/os/messages/<lang>` (`ar`, `de`, `es`, `fr`, `ja`, `ko`, `nl`, `pt`, `ru`, `tr`, `zh-tw`) is a pack of every sentence the shell, Settings, Files, the install sheet and Applications and access write, in the formal register the assistant's packs use. A host passes one as `messages` to `mountDesktop`, `settingsModule` and `filesModule` (the last two also take `locale`, so Settings, Files and "This browser" carry their names in that language); with Arabic and `dir: 'rtl'` the desktop mirrors. A browser check renders a sentinel pack and finds no chrome text outside the catalogue, and renders every pack and finds no English sentence left.
