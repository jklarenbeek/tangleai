`@tangleai/os/testing` runs the desktop without a browser for an application's own CI: `checkSuite` reports what keeps a suite document from joining (not a suite, an id listed twice or reserved, a contract two applications provide, a page on the desktop's origin, an invalid manifest, capabilities that disagree) and warns about a required contract nothing provides; `createTestDesktop` joins the suite with consent as a person's link would (narrowed per application), serves its contracts with the test's handlers, and files and decides proposals through the inbox's own path.
